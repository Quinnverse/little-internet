# 5-minute moderated task script / 5 分钟任务脚本

For the facilitator. Read each prompt aloud; do not teach the answer. Observe
silently and note where the participant hesitates or asks for help. Time each task.

| # | Say (EN) | 说（ZH） | Success signal | Time |
|---|----------|---------|----------------|------|
| 1 | "Create your own space. Give it a name and move in." | "创建你自己的空间，起个名字并搬进去。" | Room screen visible with home name | __ s |
| 2 | "Write one note that only you should ever see." | "写一条只应该你自己看到的记录。" | A new entry exists and is marked Private | __ s |
| 3 | "Add a photo and arrange your home page however you like." | "添加一张照片，按你喜欢的样子布置主页。" | Image block added; at least one block moved | __ s |
| 4 | "Make one thing public, publish it, and share the link with me." | "把一条内容设为公开，发布它，并把链接分享给我。" | A working `/@username/` URL shared | __ s |
| 5 | "Open that link in a private window. What can a visitor see?" | "在无痕窗口打开这个链接，访客能看到什么？" | Public entry visible; private entry absent; participant says so | __ s |
| 6 | "Download your website and tell me what you could do with the ZIP." | "下载你的网站，说说你能拿这个 ZIP 做什么。" | ZIP downloaded; participant mentions hosting elsewhere | __ s |

After the tasks, ask the feedback questions in `feedback-template.md`. Do not
lead them ("Wasn't the privacy nice?"). Record exact wording of praise and confusion.

**Privacy comprehension probes** (ask after task 5, neutral wording):
- "Who can see the note you made in task 2?" / "第 2 步那条记录谁能看到？"
- "If you unpublish, what happens to the link? What about a copy someone saved?"
  / "撤销发布后链接会怎样？别人保存的副本呢？"
- "Where is the thing that lets you update or unpublish? What if you lose it?"
  / "能更新或撤销发布的凭证在哪？丢了会怎样？"
