# Try Little Internet (5 minutes)

**Your corner of the internet. Yours to keep.**

Little Internet is a tiny app for your phone that lets you build a personal home
on the web. Write private notes, add photos, adopt a small ghost, and choose
exactly what visitors can see. Everything stays on your device unless you
explicitly publish it — and you can export the whole site and take it anywhere.

## What you need

- A phone with a modern browser (Chrome or Safari). No account, no email, no signup.
- About five minutes.

## Open it

Visit the app URL the organiser gave you (or the published address). On a phone,
you can "Add to Home screen" to install it like an app.

## The five tasks

1. **Make your space** — enter a name for your home and tap *Move in*.
2. **Write something just for you** — open *Diary*, add an entry. It is **Private**
   by default: only you can see it on this device.
3. **Add a photo and decorate** — open *Room*, add a photo block, move blocks around.
4. **Publish one thing** — make an entry *Public*, open *Door → Open your door
   online*, choose a short address (like `yourname`) and tap *Publish my corner*.
   You get a link you can share.
5. **Look from a visitor's side** — open the shared link in a private/incognito
   window. Confirm you see the public entry but NOT your private one.

## Good to know

- **Private by default.** A note is invisible to visitors until you open its door.
- **Your address is yours.** Keep this browser: the publish credential lives only
  here, and you need it to update or unpublish.
- **Unpublish anytime.** The link stops working immediately; copies people already
  saved cannot be recalled.
- **Portable.** Download the website ZIP from *Door* and host it on any static
  host. Your ghost, notes and recordings still work — no platform needed.

## Privacy promise

Private by default · Personal by design · Portable by nature.

No accounts, no tracking, no private data sent to the server. Only what you mark
public and publish is uploaded. See `docs/privacy-audit.md` for the technical detail.

Please fill in the feedback form after the tasks — it takes one minute.
